# Breakout Brand Engine

Content and brand operating skills modelled on how breakout disruptor brands
actually run: customer psychology, copy craft, category strategy, content
systems at volume, editor-team leadership, regulated claims, and AI-native
production.

Built for the case where all of these are one job — a content leader running
a full portfolio (paid social, brand film, out-of-home, ambassador, organic,
CRM, retail, PR) at high volume, in a category where what you say about the
product is legally constrained.

## How the skills connect

The pillars are a chain, not a menu. Insight sets what is true about the
customer; copy turns it into language; strategy decides what the brand owns;
systems ship it at volume; people make the systems run; and the regulated
layer constrains all of it.

Two skills are load-bearing across the whole set. `health-claim-gate` is
referenced from insight, copy, strategy and AI skills, because in a regulated
category claims are not a final review — they shape what you are allowed to
think about. `concept-brief` is where everything upstream becomes executable
by someone who wasn't in the room.

## A note on the examples

Brand examples throughout are illustrative, drawn from public observation of
how these companies market themselves. They are here to make mechanisms
concrete, not to serve as sourced case studies. Verify anything you intend to
cite externally.

## A note on the regulatory content

`health-claim-gate` and `localization-claims-matrix` cite public regulatory
guidance and link their sources. They describe an operating system for getting
the right things in front of the right reviewers early. They are not legal
advice, they do not replace qualified counsel or a regulatory team, and
regulatory positions change — the FDA's December 2025 signal on DSHEA
disclaimer placement is one example of why any position should be confirmed
before it is relied on.
