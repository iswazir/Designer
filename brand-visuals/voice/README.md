# IM8 Voice

`IM8_VOICE.md` is the voice guide for all IM8 copy: ads, product pages, deck copy, everything. Added 2026-09-15.

Governing line: **Know more. Say less. Prove what you say.**

Key rules that apply directly to deck and creative copy already in this repo:
- No adjectives standing in for evidence (Section 5, 26): "science-backed," "clinically proven," "premium," "advanced," "optimal" as filler — show the study, the form, the number instead.
- Candour as the premium signal (Section 8–9): state what evidence shows, what it doesn't, and stop there — this is already how the deck's claim flags and "No Small Print" concept work.
- One human line per piece, not five (Section 12).
- Desire over fear; specific futures, not generic ones (Section 13–14) — matches the "What Matters." ambassador concept's "still curious" territory over any anti-ageing framing.
- Never call anything "premium" or "world-class" — demonstrate it instead (Section 17). Applies to production notes and art-direction language too, not just customer-facing copy.

When writing or editing any IM8 copy (deck slides, ad concepts, product pages), check it against this file, especially Section 26 (banned words) and Section 30 (the eight editor tests), before calling it final.
