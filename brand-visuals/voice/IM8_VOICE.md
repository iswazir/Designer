# IM8 VOICE

## The governing principle

**Know more. Say less. Prove what you say.**

IM8 is built on serious nutritional science.

It should not sound serious about itself.

The voice is precise, composed and unusually clear. It knows the mechanisms. It knows the evidence. It knows where the evidence stops. And it knows that most people did not wake up wanting another lecture about their mitochondria.

So we do the work behind the words.

Then we use fewer of them.

**Deep science. Lightly carried.**

---

# 1. THE ESSENCE

IM8 sounds like someone who understands both the science and the person reading it.

Not a wellness influencer.

Not a biohacker.

Not a clinician performing authority.

Not a luxury brand trying to make vitamins mysterious.

An informed, curious, exacting person who has done the reading and can explain what matters without making complexity the customer's problem.

The intelligence is obvious.

The effort isn't.

That is the voice.

## Health has become too much work.

More information.

More products.

More protocols.

More things to measure.

More things to remember.

People who care most about their health can end up carrying the greatest amount of health administration.

IM8 exists on the other side of that.

Not less science.

**Less work around the science.**

Not fewer standards.

**Fewer decisions.**

Not a shortcut to health.

**A better way to practice it.**

This tension should sit underneath almost everything we make:

> **A lot goes into taking care of yourself. Taking IM8 shouldn't add to it.**

# 3. THE VOICE EQUATION

Every IM8 communication is built from four qualities.

## INTELLIGENCE

We understand the science deeply enough to make it clear.

## CANDOUR

We say what we know, what we don't, and the difference between the two.

## EASE

We remove unnecessary complexity from both the product and the language around it.

## ANTICIPATION

Health is not the destination.

There are things people still want to do with it.

Together:

> **Intelligence + Candour + Ease + Anticipation**

Take away intelligence and we become wellness marketing.

Take away candour and we become supplement advertising.

Take away ease and we become a research paper.

Take away anticipation and we become a label.

IM8 needs all four.

# 4. THE MOST IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

## We are sophisticated. Our language isn't complicated.

Scientific brands often confuse complexity with credibility.

IM8 does the opposite.

The deeper our understanding, the simpler our explanation should become.

Not:

**Pyridoxal-5'-phosphate serves as the metabolically active coenzymatic form of vitamin B6.**

Not:

**Our advanced B6 helps unlock your potential.**

Instead:

**P5P is the active form of vitamin B6, meaning your body can use it directly. Vitamin B6 supports hundreds of enzyme reactions involved in processes including energy metabolism and nervous-system function.**

We don't dumb science down.

We remove the parts that make understanding unnecessarily difficult.

**Clear is not basic.**

Clear is what happens after someone has done the work.

# 5. SCIENCE IS A VERB

Never tell people we are scientific when we can behave scientifically instead.

Don't say:

**Science-backed.**

Show the study.

Don't say:

**Clinically researched.**

Explain what was researched.

Don't say:

**Advanced formulation.**

Explain why the form was chosen.

Don't say:

**Optimal dose.**

Show the amount and rationale.

Don't say:

**Transparent.**

Publish what others might put in the small print.

The governing rule:

> **Replace prestige language with information.**

Specificity is more convincing than adjectives.

And more premium.

---

# 6. EVERY INGREDIENT HAS TO ANSWER "WHY?"

An ingredient list tells people what is inside.

IM8 should explain why it is there.

Every meaningful ingredient story moves through five questions:

### WHAT IS IT?

Name it plainly.

### WHAT DOES THE BODY USE IT FOR?

Explain the relevant biological role.

### WHY THIS FORM?

Give the formulation rationale where substantiated.

### WHY THIS AMOUNT?

Give the dose and the reasoning behind it where supportable.

### WHY DOES IT BELONG HERE?

Explain its role within the formulation.

That gives us:

> **Ingredient → mechanism → form → amount → role.**

No ingredient gets a heroic origin story simply for appearing on the label.

No ingredient becomes "powerful."

No ingredient "unlocks" anything.

The interesting part is the decision.

**Tell people why we made it.**

# 8. CANDOUR IS THE PREMIUM SIGNAL

Most brands show you their strongest evidence.

IM8 should also show you its edges.

The study size.

The duration.

Whether an outcome was self-reported.

What the study measured.

What it didn't measure.

What one study cannot establish.

What changed in the formulation.

What we're still investigating.

This doesn't weaken authority.

It demonstrates it.

### Instead of:

**95% experienced more energy.**

### IM8 says:

**95% reported more energy.**

Then:

**Reported is the important word.**

Then explain why.

That is IM8.

Confidence without theatre.

---

# 9. NO SMALL PRINT

Transparency should not live beneath the asterisk.

It should become content.

### THE RESULT

**95% reported more energy.**

### WHAT "REPORTED" MEANS

Participants answered questions about how they felt.

Energy was self-reported rather than measured with a biomarker.

### THE STUDY

12 weeks.
60 participants.
Placebo-controlled.
[Verified methodology.]

### WHAT IT TELLS US

[Precisely supported conclusion.]

### WHAT IT DOESN'T

One study is not a verdict.

It doesn't establish outcomes the study didn't measure.

And self-reported results are exactly that: reported by the people taking part.

### WHAT NOW?

We keep studying it.

**Good science leaves another question.**

---

# 10. WE DON'T SELL CERTAINTY

Science evolves.

Our language should be comfortable with that.

There is enormous authority in saying:

**We don't know yet.**

**The evidence is promising, not conclusive.**

**That's what this study found.**

**We haven't studied that outcome.**

**This is why we chose this amount.**

**We changed our mind.**

**The newer evidence was better.**

**So we changed the formula.**

A brand terrified of uncertainty sounds like marketing.

A brand comfortable with uncertainty sounds like it understands science.

No:

**We know life can feel overwhelming.**

No:

**You're exhausted from trying to do everything right.**

Instead:

**My supplement routine needed a routine.**

**This brand understands me.**

The second is what marketers want people to think.

The first is what makes them think it.

# 20. THE THREE DEPTHS OF SCIENCE

IM8 should communicate science in layers.

## LEVEL 1 — GET IT

One sentence anyone can understand.

**P5P is a form of vitamin B6 your body can use directly.**

## LEVEL 2 — UNDERSTAND IT

Explain why that matters.

**Vitamin B6 is involved in hundreds of enzyme reactions, including processes related to energy metabolism and nervous-system function.**

## LEVEL 3 — INSPECT IT

Dose.

Form.

Mechanism.

Study.

Methodology.

Limitations.

References.

People should never need Level 3 to understand Level 1.

But Level 3 should exist for anyone who wants it.

That is what confidence looks like.

# 21. THE AD SYSTEM

Every IM8 ad moves through five jobs.

Not necessarily five paragraphs.

Five jobs.

## 1. RECOGNITION

Something familiar.

**My supplement routine needed a routine.**

## 2. REFRAME

Change how the problem is understood.

**More products can mean more decisions. And more decisions make consistency harder.**

## 3. RESOLUTION

Introduce IM8 plainly.

**IM8 brings daily foundational nutrition into one morning drink.**

## 4. REASON TO BELIEVE

One proof point.

Not eight.

The one that answers the question currently in the reader's mind.

## 5. AGENCY

Return control to the person.

**Make it part of the morning.**

**Build it daily.**

**Then get on with the day.**

Emotion earns attention.

The idea creates relevance.

The product resolves it.

Evidence earns belief.

Agency closes.

# 23. THE CLAIMS RULE

The brand can understand more than it is allowed to say.

That is not a creative problem.

That is creative discipline.

> **Say what the evidence carries. Show what it doesn't.**

If evidence supports an outcome, communicate it precisely and with the required qualification.

If it doesn't, don't smuggle the claim into a beautiful sentence.

Use casting.

Behavior.

Setting.

Timing.

A look.

A pause.

A scene.

The best unsayable insight may become the best shot in the film.

That is still copywriting.

Just without copy.

---

# 24. CLAIMS SHOULD GET MORE PRECISE AS THEY GET BIGGER

Small claim:

**Contains magnesium.**

Straightforward.

Larger claim:

**Supports normal muscle function.**

Needs appropriate substantiation.

Larger still:

**Participants reported improved energy.**

Needs study context.

The bigger the implication, the closer the evidence should sit beside it.

Never make the reader travel three screens to discover the qualification that changes the meaning of the headline.

# 29. THE SIX-SECOND TEST

Before a line runs, ask:

### CAN I UNDERSTAND IT?

Immediately.

### CAN I BELIEVE IT?

The evidence is visible or available.

### CAN I FEEL IT?

There is a human reason to care.

### CAN I REPEAT IT?

The language is simple enough to remember.

### CAN WE DEFEND IT?

Every implication is supportable.

### COULD ANY OTHER SUPPLEMENT BRAND SAY IT?

If yes, it isn't finished.

---

# 30. THE EIGHT EDITOR TESTS

Every piece passes these.

## 1. Replace adjectives with evidence.

"Rigorous" becomes the protocol.

"Advanced" becomes the form.

"Powerful" disappears.

## 2. Make the science understandable.

Never scientific-sounding for its own sake.

## 3. Explain the decision.

Why this?

Why this form?

Why this amount?

## 4. Keep claims exact.

No implication larger than its evidence.

## 5. Keep humanity restrained.

One observation can do the work of a paragraph.

## 6. Leave something wanted.

Health exists in service of living.

## 7. Remove effort from the language.

Read it aloud.

If it sounds written, rewrite it.

## 8. Remove one more thing.

Then read it again.

# 31. THE NORTH STAR

The customer should never finish an IM8 piece thinking:

**That sounded scientific.**

They should think:

**Now I understand.**

They should never think:

**That brand really understands wellness.**

They should think:

**That's exactly what I've been trying to solve.**

They should never think:

**This will change my life.**

They should think:

**I could actually keep doing this.**

And they should never feel that health is being sold to them as another project.

The product should feel like the opposite.

A lot of thinking.

A lot of science.

A lot of decisions already made.

So the person taking it has one fewer to make.
