## Slide 10 — The Creative Work I
**Kicker:** 05 THE CREATIVE WORK
**Headline:** Five Concepts.
**Subtitle (italic):** Two Jobs Shown Here.
**Standfirst:** Each removes a different barrier between attention and durable growth.

**Card 1 — The Failure Trap**
- Target: adults ~35–55 already trying to take care of their health, juggling trackers/apps/supplements.
- Hook: "I started trying to take care of myself. Somehow, I gave myself another thing I could fail at."
- Tension · Format · Audience: overwhelm from health optimization · creator 9:16, static, CRM, AppLovin · broad self-improvement audience.
- Storyboard (6 frames): browser tabs (phone-camera feel) → cluttered cabinet of generic pill bottles → notification/planner montage → phone face-down, morning light → sachet tears, crimson bloom → "ONE PLACE TO BEGIN."
- Claim flag: no disease/fatigue/sleep/digestion/recovery/cognition claim; only approved product line is "One place to begin."

**Card 2 — Formulated for the Fact Checkers. / No Small Print**
- Target: skeptical, category-experienced supplement buyers.
- Hook: "An ingredient list is not a formula."
- Tension · Format · Audience: category skepticism needs inspection, not another promise · organic carousel, Reels, PDP, CRM · research-driven buyers.
- Shown as: single carousel-cover frame — Card 1 "SLEEP IS NOT ONE INGREDIENT," pagination "1/7," corner-peek of Card 2. Note: "BUILT AS A CLICK-THROUGH · SEVEN CARDS, ONE FRAME · FULL SEQUENCE IN APPENDIX."
- Claim flag: no "synergy," "clinically proven," "best absorbed," or product-specific claim before Approved Content Menu exists.
