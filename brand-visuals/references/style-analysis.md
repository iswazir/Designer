# IM8 brand expression system: the standards world

This file is the visual brief every prompt inherits. It defines the world IM8 lives in when no ambassador is in the frame. The test for every frame: remove the ambassador, does it still read IM8?

Sources: the Part 3 concept build v2; the competitive ad library (tmrw, Timeline, Seed, Superpower, captured 1 September 2026); two Pinterest reference boards; the original hero-image brief; IM8's public positioning. IM8's site could not be fetched in this environment. Replace or extend if a brand guide becomes available.

## The idea in one line

> A standards world, not a ritual world.

AG1 and the category sell the morning, upgraded: comfort, habit, warmth, green. IM8's truths are discipline, a placebo-controlled trial of the finished formula, third-party testing, measured bodies. Those are standards truths. The world that expresses them looks like precision instruments, architecture, materials and measurement, with light that reveals rather than flatters. Closer to a Leica or a Swiss watchmaker than to a supplement brand.

## Five rules

1. **One light source.** Directional, cool, from one side. Warmth is an edge of sun, never a wash. No fill, no ring light, no beauty dish.
2. **Materials before faces.** Slate, concrete, brushed steel, frosted and clear glass, water, condensation, skin texture. A face appears only when it is doing something. It never smiles at camera.
3. **Crimson is the exception.** One crimson object per frame, under ten percent of the frame. The red glass is the recurring distinctive asset. Green appears only as the thing IM8 stands apart from.
4. **Stillness.** Locked-off or slow. Premium holds. Motion blur only in the environment, never on the subject.
5. **Nothing styled.** Real surfaces, real clutter, real light, real dawn. Precision comes from the frame and the light, not from set dressing.

## Palette

| Role | Values | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Field | Steel blue, charcoal, slate, smoked glass, concrete grey, pale sky, soft stone, bone | Environments, apparel, surfaces |
| Warmth | One edge of early sun, skin | Light only, never a colour cast |
| Exception | IM8 crimson | The liquid, a rule on white, a shoe detail. One per frame. |
| Forbidden field | Green as environment or product colour | Only inside Out of the Green, as the field IM8 leaves |

## Recurring distinctive assets

These are the things the market should learn to retrieve as IM8 without a logo.

- **The red glass.** A single clear glass of crimson liquid, one hard light, condensation. Shot like fragrance, not like a supplement.
- **The cloud.** Crimson entering clear water, macro, the moment it blooms.
- **The crimson rule.** A single thin horizontal line on white or bone. The evidence grammar across cards, PDP and CRM.
- **Dawn architecture.** Long urban sightlines, cool shadow, one warm break of sun between buildings.
- **Hands.** Tendons, texture, a precise action. Tearing, pouring, holding. Never a face for its own sake.
- **Breath.** Cold air, 5am, a profile.

## Light

One source. Cool, directional, raking where it can. Dawn for exteriors. A window for interiors, shot from the side. Flat fluorescent only when the concept calls for honesty about an office or a break room, and then shot exactly as it is. Deep, detailed shadows. Fine analog grain. Restrained saturation. Crisp natural highlights.

## Lens and distance

Slightly too close. 35mm equivalent for people, macro for materials and the cloud, locked-off for plates. Faces sharp, backgrounds honest. Shallow depth of field only to isolate the one crimson object.

## Casting

Adults 35 to 70. A person with a job and a life. Expressions neutral, focused, or tired with dignity. Never posed. No exaggerated musculature. Athletes and ambassadors are shot the same way as nurses: same lens, same light, same distance. If the ambassador is removed and the frame stops reading as IM8, the frame has failed.

## Product

The sachet and bottle are objects with weight. Studio, one light, dark slate or pale stone ground, crimson liquid as the hero material. Never generated; real packs are composited over blank placeholders. Never in the same frame as an injury, treatment table or medical assessment. Never a beauty shot before the last frame of a sequence.

## Typography and documents

Not generated. Cards are white or bone with the crimson rule, set in Figma with the brand type, small and restrained, numbers treated as design objects with their qualifiers attached. Documents are photographed real documents composited onto generated plates with plate text left illegible.

## What the world never does

- The green pour and the greens-powder morning ritual.
- Smiling at camera. Anyone.
- The ingredient constellation, the anatomical cutaway, the biology diagram as hero art.
- The elite athlete as the aspirational peak.
- The school run and the supermom.
- The before-and-after reveal.
- Warm fill, ring light, beauty dish, CGI gloss, plastic skin, stock fitness posing.
- Fast cutting.
