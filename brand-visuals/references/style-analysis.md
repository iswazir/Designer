# Style analysis: IM8 concept visuals

Sources: the Part 3 concept build v2; the competitive ad library (tmrw, Timeline, Seed, Superpower, captured 1 September 2026); two Pinterest reference boards (informational design, editorial health); the original hero-image brief. IM8's site could not be fetched in this environment, so brand-side facts are from the concept build and public positioning, not a scrape. Replace or extend this file if a brand guide becomes available.

## The one-line grammar

> Observed, not performed. Documents photographed, not drawn. Crimson as the exception in a cool, quiet field.

## Palette

| Role | Values | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Field | Steel blue, charcoal, smoked glass, concrete grey, pale sky, soft stone, bone | Environments, apparel, surfaces |
| Warmth | Restrained early sunlight, skin, wood | Light source only, never a colour wash |
| Exception | IM8 crimson | One object per frame: the liquid, a shoe detail, a rule on a card. Under ten percent of frame. |
| Forbidden field | Green in any quantity as environment or product colour | The category is green. IM8 is not. Green appears only as the thing IM8 stands apart from, in Out of the Green. |

## Light

Natural, directional, honest. Morning window light for kitchens. Flat fluorescent for offices and break rooms, shot as it is, not corrected. Dawn for streets. No ring light, no beauty dish, no rim light on people. Deep but detailed shadows. Fine analog grain. Restrained saturation.

## Lens and distance

Slightly too close. 35mm equivalent for people, handheld for creator frames, locked off for plates. Faces sharp, backgrounds honest. Shallow depth of field only where the concept needs a single object isolated (Out of the Green, product still life).

## Casting

Adults 35 to 70. A person with a job and a life, not a persona. Expressions neutral, focused, or tired with dignity. Never smiling at camera. Never posed. No exaggerated musculature. Athletes are shot the same way as nurses: same lens, same light, same distance.

## Environments

Lived-in kitchens with real clutter. Offices with the dull afternoon left in. Hotel rooms. Break rooms. Suburban roads at dawn. Plain gyms with no branding. Ordinary clinics. Nothing styled. Nothing aspirational as a set.

## Product presence

The product appears as a glass of crimson liquid, a pour, or a plain blank sachet placeholder. Never generated packaging. Never a beauty shot before the last frame of a sequence. Never in the same frame as an injury, a treatment table or a medical assessment.

## Typography and documents

Not generated. Cards are white with a single crimson rule, set in Figma. Documents (registry record, label, certificate of analysis, printed reports) are photographed real documents, composited onto generated desk or table plates with the text left illegible in the plate.

## What we deliberately reject (the category's clichés)

- The green pour. The greens-powder morning ritual.
- The ingredient constellation graphic.
- Biology diagrams and anatomical cutaways as hero art.
- The elite athlete as the aspirational peak.
- The school run and the supermom.
- The before-and-after reveal.
- Plastic skin, CGI gloss, stock fitness posing.
